I'm Jnana, a full-stack and AI engineer from Odisha, India. I work across the stack, from databases and backends to frontends and deployment, and on the AI systems in between: LLM apps, retrieval, voice agents and computer vision.

I spent a year at Skillablers in Bangalore shipping production AI systems: a real-time voice agent, a self-hosted question-answering assistant and computer-vision features. I'm also the AI & DevOps engineer of a product team building Swaram, a voice AI platform for small Indian businesses. I'm AWS Certified as a Cloud Practitioner and an AI Practitioner.
